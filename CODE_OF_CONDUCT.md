# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and sharing others' private information (including locations from logs or streams) are
not tolerated.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact options on
[github.com/ramazansancar](https://github.com/ramazansancar). Reports are handled confidentially.

Bu proje Contributor Covenant 2.1 davranış kurallarını izler: saygılı ve yapıcı ol, kimsenin kişisel bilgisini
(loglardaki ya da yayınlardaki konumlar dahil) paylaşma.
